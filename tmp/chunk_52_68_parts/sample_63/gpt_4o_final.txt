1. **The Franklin Feast**

3. **Liberty Flavors**

   - **Aggregate Rating:** 4.8

4. **Penn's Landing Delights**

5. **Historic Taste**

### Casual Dining Options:
6. **Marukame Udon**

7. **Red Mesa Cantina**  
   - **Average Cost:** $36

8. **Hippopotamus - Museum Hotel**
